# Treatment series

A treatment series is a course of several visits: a physiotherapy
prescription for six sessions, a run of radiation, a course of injections,
chemotherapy cycles, a dental treatment plan. It answers what a single visit
cannot: when the course started and ended, on which days each session took
place, who ordered it, who gave it, and which condition it was for.

## Starting one

Open **Care**, then **Visits**. **New series** sits beside **Treatment
series**, above the appointments. A series needs a name and a start day;
everything else is optional:

- **Sessions**: how many the prescription or plan covers. Leave it empty
  for a course with no fixed number.
- **Valid until**: the last day it may run. A German _Heilmittelverordnung_,
  for example, has to start within 28 days and lapses after a longer break;
  enter the day your therapist names.
- **Ordered by** and **Given by**: two entries from your address book. A
  practice rather than a person usually fits "given by", since the therapist
  changes from session to session.
- **Condition**: the illness it treats, when the illness module is on.

## Sessions

The sessions are ordinary visits. Open a series and use **Add session**: the
visit form opens with the series, its kind and its practice filled in. A
date in the future books the appointment, with its reminder and its place in
the care calendar; a date in the past files it as done. Any visit can also be
put into a series, or taken out of one, from its own form.

HealthLog counts from the visits, every time it shows a series:

- **3 of 6**: sessions that took place. A cancelled or missed appointment
  does not count and does not get a number.
- **Not started** until the first session took place, then **Running**.
- **Completed** once every planned session took place, or once you used
  **End series**. **Reopen** undoes that.
- **Expired** when the last valid day has passed with sessions left.

In the week before the last valid day, if sessions are still left, you get
one reminder on the channels you chose for check-up reminders, in the morning on
your own clock. Moving the last valid day can bring a new one; a series that
is ended, completed or without a number of sessions gets none.

Each visit of a series shows its place in it, such as "Session 3 of 6".

## Where else it shows

- **Timeline**: the series is a bar in the visits lane, from its first
  session to its end, with the sessions as points.
- **Doctor report**: a table of the series in the report's window, with the
  sessions, the period, who ordered and gave it, and the condition. It comes
  with **Visits**.
- **Care calendar**: a booked session is titled "Session 3 of 6" (with
  descriptive titles), and the last valid day of a running series is an
  all-day event. The series' name never reaches the calendar.
- **Search** finds the sessions of a series by the series' name.
- **Backups** carry the series and which visit belongs to which.

Deleting a series keeps its visits; they only stop being grouped. The undo
right after brings the grouping back.
