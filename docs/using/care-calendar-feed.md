# Subscribing to your appointments and care dates

HealthLog can publish your booked visits, the check-ups coming due and your
vaccination boosters as a calendar subscription, so they show up in the
calendar app you already use, next to everything else. The feed is computed
fresh every time your calendar app asks for it; HealthLog stays the only place
the data is kept.

## Turning it on

Open **Settings**, then **Health record**, and find **Care calendar
subscription** under **Calendar**. Before the button, the card says what it
means: your calendar provider stores the dates on its servers, and turning the
subscription off later does not remove copies it already holds. HealthLog asks
you to confirm it is you (a password or passkey) and then shows the link once.
Copy it, or use **Open in calendar app**.

Only you can turn it on, and only for your own record. Someone you have shared
your record with, or the guardian of a managed profile, gets a feed of their
own dates, never yours. It is managed on the web.

Adding it works as for the cycle feed: see
[Subscribing to your cycle forecast](cycle-calendar-feed.md#adding-it-to-your-calendar).

## What the feed shows

- **Booked visits**: every visit you booked for the future, at its time, as a
  one-hour event. A visit stores a start, not an end, so the hour is a
  placeholder.
- **Check-ups coming due**: every check-up you set up under Check-ups, as an
  all-day event on the day it comes due. This is the same due day the
  Check-ups page shows, so a skip, a snooze or a "done" moves it in your
  calendar too.
- **Vaccination boosters coming due**, the same way. They follow the
  vaccinations module: with it switched off, they are left out.

The feed reaches 30 days back, so a date does not vanish the moment it
passes, and about 13 months ahead. Each of the three kinds can be switched off
on the card.

Titles are discreet by default: every event is a dot (●), and the calendar is
called "HealthLog". Descriptive titles name the practitioner of a visit
("Appointment: Practice Lindenhof") and the label of a check-up ("Due: Skin
check"). Events never carry a visit's reason, outcome or body site, a
check-up's location, a note, or anything else from your record.

## Turning it off

**Turn off** ends the link. Remove the subscribed calendar in your calendar
app too: what it already fetched stays there until you do. For a new link,
turn the subscription off and on again.
